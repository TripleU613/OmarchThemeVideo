# Ideas: one per scene

Every scene gets one idea: a visual metaphor for its line, with a setup, a turn
on the key word and a payoff. Not the wordmark, not a UI screenshot.

## Rules for every scene

1. **One idea.** A metaphor the viewer feels, not a picture of the words.
2. **The three choruses escalate.** Chorus 1 is one person and one machine.
   Chorus 2 is a crowd and a city. Chorus 3 is the world and the sky.
3. **The look stays Omarchy.** Everything is drawn as lattice light in Tokyo
   Night inks: pixel art and silhouettes, never photoreal, never clip-art.
4. **The wordmark is rationed.** It appears only on the naming lines and the
   bookends.
5. **The words are part of the picture.** Each line's words live inside its
   scene (etched, lit, painted, in the sky), each landing on its sung onset.
   They load from your lyrics file at render time.
6. **Music-only stretches are full set pieces,** not filler.
7. **Threads run through the film:** the cursor (i-01 to x-end), the paper
   plane (p1-04, v2-06, p2-04, x-o1) and the green glow.

Line descriptions below are loose paraphrases, not lyrics. Scenes marked
**keep** are already a real idea and get refined. Every other scene is a new idea.

## Verse 1: the rented world, then making it yours

- **v1-01** · *shut [line] system* · **Walled garden.** From above, a white hedge maze. One green cell (you) hits a dead end on each word, and every path ends at a padlock. The camera rises: the maze is shaped like a phone's app grid.
- **v1-02** · *a pretty screen run by someone else* · **Puppet strings.** A glossy UI where every element hangs from strings. On each word the camera tilts up the strings toward the hands that own them.
- **v1-03** · *every click costs you* · **The meter is running.** Every click drops a coin, a taxi meter rolls, and anything you stop paying for goes dark.
- **v1-04** · *ripping it out, building new* · **keep:** the white world tears like paper, Tokyo Night shows underneath, and new cells build in the gap.
- **v1-05** · *working late at night* · **Rain on the window.** Rain streaks down black glass with city bokeh behind it, and code reflected on the glass mixes with the drops. One glow [line].
- **v1-06** · *making your own Linux* · **The forge.** An anvil [line]. The hammer strikes on each word, sparks fly as cells, and the glowing piece takes the shape of a machine.
- **v1-07** · *not a clone [line] one* · **The photocopier.** [line] goes through a copier, and copies of copies come out degrading. The last sheet comes out as an original.
- **v1-08** · *this one belongs to you* · **keep:** fingerprint enrollment around the mark.

## Chorus 1: one person, one machine

- **c1-01** · *the hook: fixing anything* · **Kintsugi.** A cracked bowl. Green light flows into one crack per word until it's whole and glowing.
- **c1-02** · *building the ideal machine* · **Exploded view.** A machine hovers in pieces. The parts slam together, one click per word, and it powers on.
- **c1-03** · *giving it its name* · **keep:** the letterpress name, set syllable by syllable.
- **c1-04** · *showing it to everyone* · **The first viewer.** Over the shoulder, one person turns the screen toward a friend, and the glow lights both faces.
- **c1-05** · *prettier and improved* · **Sketch to real.** A pencil wireframe of a desktop on paper becomes the finished glowing render, word by word.
- **c1-06** · *free but tidy* · **keep:** the merge sort, from a wild shuffle to clean order.
- **c1-07** · *the hook again* · **Heartbeat.** A flatline on a monitor, a thump on each word, and on the last word the green heartbeat returns.
- **c1-08** · *the title line* · **The unveiling.** A machine under a cloth on a stage. The cloth is pulled away on the key word and the machine glows.
